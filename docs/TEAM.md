## What it is

Volunteer Copilot helps a small volunteer group turn a messy event note into a roster, fill the gaps, and keep students safe by always messaging them in a group that includes their advisor. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

## Done and working (demo mode)

- Note to draft roster, publish, no-account sign-up link, live roster with gaps
- "Fill this gap" suggests who to ask; students always get one group message that includes the advisor
- Drop-out and no-show backfill drafts, club contact list, advisor tap-to-confirm service hours
- 62 automated tests, plus a security review and accessibility fixes

## Not done (say so plainly)

- The demo keeps data on one device and uses simple rules, not real AI
- The live version (Netlify, Supabase, AI service) is written but has never run for real
- Real texting is not built, and no coordinator has been interviewed yet
